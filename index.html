Circle of Fifths

Neighbors on the circle sound good together. Click a key to move there.

Click the grid to add notes. Drag to move, drag the right edge to stretch, click a note again to delete. Ctrl+drag selects several: then drag, arrow keys, Ctrl+C/V/D, Delete. Alt+click = accent (louder), Vel on a track = velocity lane. Shift+click makes a Scale note ♯/♭.

Bangerator

Make music with numbers instead of note names.
Pick a scale, put numbers on the grid, swap scales to hear the same loop in a new mood, then arrange your patterns into a song.

All sound is made right here in your browser.
